              • Emergency Power & Backup Systems:<\/strong> Ensuring reliable power supply and backup systems to support critical medical equipment and life-saving services.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
              • Accessibility & Wayfinding:<\/strong> Creating a clear and easy-to-navigate environment for patients, visitors, and staff.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n<\/li>\n\n\n\n
